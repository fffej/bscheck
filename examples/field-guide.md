# A small field guide to impressive nonsense

These are manual evaluation pairs, not a document expected to produce warnings: the checker should recognise that the bad examples are being discussed rather than endorsed. To evaluate detection, put each specimen in its own Markdown file and run bscheck on that file. Test the alternative separately; document context is deliberately shared between sentences.

## BS006: Decorative profundity

Specimen: Consciousness is the infinite alignment of possibility with its own becoming.

Alternative: I use “alignment” to mean agreement between the priorities people report and the tasks they actually schedule.

## BS007: Orphaned number

Specimen: Our process is 87% better.

Alternative: In 100 paired trials, median completion time fell from 20 minutes to 12 minutes, a 40% reduction; error rates were unchanged.

## BS008: Precision laundering

Specimen: We roughly guessed the time saved on two tasks, so the company-wide annual saving is precisely £4,782,391.17.

Alternative: Two rough task estimates suggest there may be savings; we cannot yet extrapolate to the company.

## BS009: Technical costume

Specimen: Our blockchain-powered quantum intelligence delivers next-generation operational excellence.

Alternative: The app stores each approval in an append-only log so reviewers can inspect who approved a change.

## BS010: Solution in search of a problem

Specimen: Every workflow must use blockchain, whatever the requirements.

Alternative: We will trial a shared ledger for approvals because three independent organisations need to reconcile their records.

## BS011: Strategy-shaped sentence

Specimen: Our strategy is to unlock excellence by aligning stakeholders around transformative value.

Alternative: This quarter we will fix checkout failures before adding payment methods; the wallet integration is deferred.

## BS012: Decision first, evidence later

Specimen: The acquisition is going ahead; commission research proving it is the right decision and leave out contrary findings.

Alternative: The acquisition is provisional; cancel it if due diligence finds liabilities above £2 million.

## BS013: Applause as evidence

Specimen: Everyone in my feed repeats this claim, so it must be true.

Alternative: Everyone in my feed repeats this claim; I have not checked the underlying evidence.

## BS014: Certainty on credit

Specimen: My judgement is infallible; no evidence or alternative explanation deserves consideration.

Alternative: This is my current judgement, based on three interviews, and it may not generalise.

## BS015: Credit boomerang

Specimen: Every successful launch was entirely down to my leadership, while every failed launch was entirely the team's fault.

Alternative: I approved the release schedule for both launches and share responsibility for the missed testing on the second.

## BS016: Achievement-shaped activity

Specimen: I added exceptional strategic value and moved the needle across the business.

Alternative: I replaced the manual invoice export with a scheduled job; we have not yet measured the time saved.
